# Business Model: Butcher Shop Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7511`
- ISCO-08: `7511`
- Occupation: Butchers, Fishmongers and Related Food Preparers
- Social impact: food-safety, worker-safety, production-continuity

## Customer

- butcher shops / fishmongers / delicatessens
- independent butcher/fishmonger crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- task/batch/progress-record logging
- meat/fish-stock supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to shop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a preparation-execution decision (e.g. a
  specific cutting or butchering operation), ever
- no direct finalization of a food-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a batch fit for sale), ever
- no override of a shop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (cut hazard, hygiene-compliance concern,
  contamination-risk concern) always route to human sign-off, regardless
  of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
